Professor Demo Interpretation

Product-facing explanation of the ERP Twin intelligence layer

1. Monitor

The ERP Twin monitors projects, datasets, models, experiments, purchases, invoices, equipment reservations, and publication lineage.

2. Detect

The Data Quality Firewall detects missing ownership, outdated model lineage, budget violations, equipment conflicts, and reproducibility gaps.

3. Govern

AI agents create proposals with confidence and evidence, while high-risk changes remain human-approved before the ERP state is modified.

Core demo message: This is not a conventional ERP dashboard. It is an AI Lab Mission Control system that combines lab operations, research lineage, data-quality risk detection, and human-supervised agentic decision support.
