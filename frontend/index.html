GOVERNANCE MODEL

Monitor · Detect · Govern

The counterintuitive product idea: the AI is not the boss. The AI is a controlled intelligence layer that must expose evidence, uncertainty, and risk before action.
1
Monitor the lab as a twin

Projects, datasets, models, experiments, purchases, invoices, equipment, and publications are monitored as one connected research-operation system.

2
Detect broken trust chains

Missing ownership, outdated datasets, budget exceptions, equipment conflicts, and reproducibility gaps become visible before they damage decisions.

3
Govern agentic action

AI agents can recommend, explain, and prioritize. Humans approve high-risk state changes. The ERP Twin becomes auditable instead of mysterious.
